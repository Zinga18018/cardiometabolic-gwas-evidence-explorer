Gene prioritisation

L2G is the source's machine-learning score. Distance is measured to the canonical transcription start site (TSS), among returned candidates only.

The candidate set is filtered by Open Targets. “Nearest returned candidate” is not the nearest gene across the genome; agreement with the L2G ranking is not a measure of accuracy.

Provenance and interpretation

Collection

Strongest reported signals selected deterministically; lead variants on the same chromosome separated by at least 1 Mb. This is a bounded, biased convenience sample, not a genome-wide benchmark. Spatial separation does not establish statistical independence.

What this project adds

Reproducible retrieval, response hashes, completeness checks, probability validation, missing-evidence handling, TSS distances and an interactive evidence audit.

No new GWAS, fine mapping, Mendelian randomisation or expression analysis was run. Derived evidence is pending human review.
